# Decision Policy — Project Generator Desktop

The decision process follows the **EvoPilot-inspired** pattern of stable IDs, explicit alternatives and verified approval states, with standard spelling.

- Active register: [`docs/decisions/index.md`](docs/decisions/index.md)
- Operating rules: [`docs/decisions/governance.md`](docs/decisions/governance.md)
- Record template: [`docs/decisions/decision-template.md`](docs/decisions/decision-template.md)

A proposal is not an accepted implementation. Accepted Discovery records may reflect user-selected directions without claiming engineering, security or release approval. Do not invent decision records or retrospective history for generated projects.
